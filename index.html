The official tabletop gaming club of the University of Canterbury. We encourage the hobby of role-playing games, board games, war games, and card games.

What Do We Do?


The aim of SAGA is to encourage the hobby of gaming including role-playing games, war games, board games, and card games. Every week, from February to December, we meet on Tuesday and Thursday evenings from 6pm till late to play board games and RPGs and more.

When We Meet


We meet every week on Tuesdays & Thursdays in Jack Erskine 244 from 6pm till 10pm, with rooms available for TTRPG groups. We also have Rehua 102 booked on Saturdays, from 9am till 10pm.
